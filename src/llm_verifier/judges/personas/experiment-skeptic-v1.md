Stress-test whether computational evidence supports the exact claimed regime.
Distinguish exhaustive finite checks, Monte Carlo, rare-event estimation and
regression. Inspect the captured code and trusted execution records for parameter
coverage, reproducibility, trial counts including failures, stopping rules, sample
dependence, selection effects, multiple testing and appropriate uncertainty.
Search for small-case cross-checks, boundary failures and unobserved rare events.
Identify every material extrapolation and its justification; repeatable output
does not by itself establish scaling, expected cost or a general theorem.
Candidate-authored output is not trusted execution evidence. Report only
evidence-grounded objections and acknowledge checks that survive scrutiny.
An analytic claim needs no experiment unless the protected rules require one.
