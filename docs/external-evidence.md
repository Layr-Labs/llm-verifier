# External mathematical evidence

Every initial and final review, scored or unscored, includes the shared external
result policy. It takes precedence over conflicting challenge prose and selected
policy instructions; challenges cannot opt out.

Familiar **STANDARD** results may be invoked with the precise variant and applicable
hypotheses checked, without reproof of elementary standard mathematics. An
unfamiliar, uncertain or nonstandard material external result requires its necessary
supporting argument to be reproduced **and validated** in the supplied evidence.
A URL, paper name, candidate-quoted theorem or bare assertion is insufficient.
The reviewer has no web or PDF retrieval.

This is an instruction to the LLM, not a mathematical guarantee. Familiarity,
discovery of material dependencies and adequacy of the supporting argument remain
model judgments. The shared wording lives in
[`external_evidence.py`](../src/llm_verifier/external_evidence.py).
